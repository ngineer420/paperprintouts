Privacy

Every generator on this site runs in your browser. Nothing you type — a class roster, a list of names, a set of measurements — is transmitted anywhere, because there is no server to transmit it to. The site is static files.

Your last-used settings are stored in your browser's local storage so the page opens the way you left it. That data never leaves the machine, and clearing your browser data removes it.

This site shows advertising through Google AdSense, which may set cookies and use them to personalise the advertising you see. You can control that through Google's own ad settings.
